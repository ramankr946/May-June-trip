Granada

5 – 8 June · 3 nights · centrepiece
The emotional peak

At 738m altitude it's cooler than Seville and Málaga — a genuine relief. The Alhambra is in a category of its own. The Albaicín is the most atmospheric urban neighbourhood in Andalusia. Free tapas with every drink at every bar. Three tight, full nights — Alhambra the day after arrival, Albaicín the day after that, drive to Madrid the morning after.

Where to stay

Plaza Nueva / Cathedral

Historic centre within walking distance of Plaza Nueva — gateway to both the Alhambra hill and the Albaicín. Avoid the Albaicín itself for accommodation with a toddler — the lanes are steep and impractical with luggage. Hotel parking essential.

  • Marriott Bonvoy property · confirm parking
  • Parador de Granada if available — sits inside the Alhambra grounds, unlike anywhere else

Mérida

10 – 11 June · 1 night
UNESCO Roman Iberia · the surprise stop
EMERITA AVGVSTA · 25 BC
Capital of Roman Lusitania

Founded as Emerita Augusta in 25 BC, capital of Roman Lusitania, and home to the most extensive and best-preserved Roman ruins in all of Spain. The 790-metre Puente Romano is the longest surviving Roman bridge in the world. The theatre still hosts performances every summer. Compact, walkable, almost tourist-free, half the price of the big cities — and exactly halfway between Madrid and Lisbon.

Where to stay

Compact historic centre

Stay within walking distance of Plaza de España. Roman Theatre 5 min, Temple of Diana 2 min, Roman Bridge 8 min. Driving the centre is hard (very narrow streets); pick a hotel with parking.

  • Parador de Mérida — converted 18th-century convent, best in the city · parking
  • Ilunión Mérida Palace — central on Plaza de España · Moorish decor
  • AC Hotel Mérida Palacio — Marriott Bonvoy · central · simpler
