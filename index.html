Your Portugal base

Costa del Sol

2 – 5 June · 3 nights · Estepona resort base
The recovery stop

Three nights of beach resort to break up an otherwise constantly-moving trip. After 6 days of driving, biometrics, jet lag, and Andalusian heat, this is where the family pace genuinely resets. Marriott's Playa Andaluza sits on the Estepona shoreline, ~30 km west of Marbella town. You skip the Picasso/old-town deep dive in favour of pools, sand, and sleep — and the trip is healthier for it. Estepona is also the closest base to Manilva (only 10–15 min west), so the Roman baths day trip becomes a short hop, not an expedition.

Where to stay · ✓ BOOKED

Marriott's Playa Andaluza, Estepona

Marriott Vacation Club property on the Costa del Sol — apartment-style accommodations with full kitchens, multiple pools, direct beach access. ~10–15 min drive to Manilva (huge advantage for the Roman baths day trip), ~30 min to Marbella old town, ~1h to Málaga centre. Free parking. 2–5 Jun · 3 nights · €1,234 total.

  • Vacation Club property — Bonvoy points/elite credit rules differ from standard Marriott hotels; check at check-in
  • Expect a timeshare sales pitch at arrival — politely decline once and they leave you alone
  • Request a sea-view or garden-view apartment when you arrive

Granada

5 – 7 June · 2 nights · Alhambra-focused
The morning everything turns on

At 738m altitude it's cooler than Seville and Marbella — a genuine relief. The Alhambra is in a category of its own, and with the morning entry locked in, the whole stop is built around it. Two nights covers the essentials cleanly: arrive afternoon for the iconic Mirador San Nicolás sunset, big morning at the palace, guided Albaicín tour through the Moorish quarter at sunset, free tapas evening to celebrate, drive out the next morning. With the booked Albaicín tour, you don't actually lose anything by trimming to 2 nights — you just get a more guided version of the neighbourhood walk.

Where to stay · ✓ BOOKED

Hotel Alhambra Palace · on the Alhambra hill

1910 grande dame inspired by the Nasrid Palace, refurbished 2017 · sits on the Alhambra hill 5–10 min walk from the palace entrance · panoramic terrace over Granada (one of the great sunset spots in the city) · on-site restaurant Príncipe · parking on site · public bus from the hotel entrance every 10 min into the city centre, 800m to downtown. 5–7 Jun · 2 nights · €1,150 total.

  • Not a Marriott property — no Bonvoy points, chosen for proximity to the Alhambra entrance on the headline morning
  • Request a city-view or Sierra Nevada-view room at check-in
  • Cocktails on the panoramic terrace before bed — the move

✓ Alhambra — booked for 6 Jun morning

Locked. Granada arrival is therefore the afternoon before (5 Jun). Be at the gates 20 minutes before your timed Nasrid Palaces slot — security queues in June.

Madrid

7 – 10 June · 3 nights · capital interlude
Retiro, Prado, Reina Sofía, food

Three nights to do Madrid properly: arrival evening + Botín dinner, a big Retiro & Prado day, a "your own pace" day for Reina Sofía and food, then a slow morning before driving west. The Princesa Plaza area is on the west side of central Madrid — 3 min walk to Plaza de España, 15 min walk to Plaza Mayor and Sobrino de Botín, 10 min Uber to Retiro and Prado. Less expensive than the Jerónimos side, and the indoor pool at the new Marriott is a real asset for Zain after the Granada heat.

Where to stay · ✓ BOOKED

Madrid Marriott Hotel Princesa Plaza · Argüelles

Brand-new 4-star Marriott, 400 rooms + 14 suites with panoramic upper floors. Indoor pool, sauna, La Chata Bar in the lobby, La Mimosa Terrace. 3 min walk to Plaza de España, 11 min to Gran Vía, 15 min to Plaza Mayor / Sobrino de Botín, 18 min drive to airport. 7–10 Jun · 3 nights · AED 5,320. Marriott Bonvoy earning normal.

  • Request a panoramic upper-floor room — Royal Palace and Sierra de Guadarrama views are the property's signature
  • Self-parking on-site (cheaper than valet) — confirm rate at check-in
  • Use Uber/Bolt for Retiro & Prado days · ~10 min each way, <€10 per ride
